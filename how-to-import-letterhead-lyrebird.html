How to Import Your Letterhead for Referral Letters in Lyrebird Health

Step-by-Step Instructions

Overview: This guide shows you how to import your clinic's custom letterhead into Lyrebird Health, so that referral letters generated through Best Practice are automatically formatted with your branding — ready to send without any manual formatting.

Part 1: Importing Your Letterhead

  1. Go to Customize → BP Referral Letters → Import a Referral Letter. Lyrebird will automatically search for the most recent referral letter sent from the patient's record.
  2. Review the letter that Lyrebird has captured. If it contains your clinic's letterhead, you're ready to proceed. If not, click Choose Another Letter to browse your most recently sent referral letters.
  3. Once you've identified the letter with your correct letterhead, click Use as a Template.
  4. Give your template a clear name — for example, Gordon Street Medical Centre — so it's easy to identify later.
  5. Press Next. Lyrebird will take a moment to import the template and will then automatically map each section of your letterhead, including:
    • Patient's medical problems
    • Medications
    • Allergies
    • Clinician account details
  6. Once the import is complete, press Save and Exit.
  7. If you have multiple users in your organisation, you can share this template with others at the same clinic so everyone benefits from the same branded letterhead.

Part 2: Using Your Template to Write a Referral

  1. Open a consult and go to Consult → Obtain Consent, then start listening.
  2. Verbalise the reason for referral during the consult. For example: "I want to refer you to a podiatrist for an annual foot check to assess blood flow and protective sensation in your feet."
  3. Press Stop Listening. Lyrebird will capture your consult notes.
  4. When ready to write the referral, go to Search for Documents, type the relevant specialty, then click Write.
  5. Instead of selecting the default BP Referral Letter or Letter to Specialist, choose the template you uploaded (e.g. Gordon Street Medical Centre).
  6. Lyrebird will generate a complete referral letter using your clinic's letterhead. From here you can:
    • Choose the addressee
    • Include relevant investigations
    • Save directly to the Correspondence app
Pro Tip: Once your template is saved, it will be used automatically every time you generate a referral — no manual formatting needed. Share it across your clinic so all users produce consistent, branded letters.

Key Benefits of This Workflow

  • Import your clinic's letterhead once and reuse it for every referral
  • Lyrebird automatically maps patient details, medications, allergies, and more
  • No manual formatting or copy-pasting required
  • Shareable across all users within the same clinic
  • Referral letters save directly to the Correspondence app, ready to send
