How to Create Referral Letters Using Your Best Practice Template

Step-by-Step Instructions

Overview: Once your consultation is complete, Lyrebird can generate multiple referral letters simultaneously — each tailored to the specific specialist — and save them directly to Correspondence Out in Best Practice. This eliminates manual copy-pasting and significantly reduces the time spent on referral documentation.
  1. Complete your consultation as normal, then click Stop Listening. Lyrebird will generate a summary of your consultation within seconds.
  2. Review the consultation summary. Lyrebird will have identified all referrals required based on the discussion. For example, for a patient with diabetes, you may see referrals flagged for:
    • Diabetes educator
    • Dietician
    • Optometrist
    • Podiatrist
  3. Click Write Document and use the search bar to find your referral letter types. Search for each specialty and select it under the BP Referral Letter option. For example:
    • Search "podiatrist" → select Podiatrist under BP Referral Letter
    • Search "dietician" → select Dietician under BP Referral Letter
    • Search "diabetes educator" → select Diabetes Educator under BP Referral Letter
    • Search "optometrist" → select Optometrist under BP Referral Letter
  4. Once all referral types are selected, click Create. Lyrebird will generate all four referral letters simultaneously — in just five clicks.
  5. Review each generated letter. Each specialty will only contain information that is relevant to their profession. For example, the podiatrist letter will reference the ingrown toenail review and diabetic foot assessment, not unrelated conditions.
  6. For each letter, select the addressee using either:
    • The Best Practice contact list (available via the Lyrebird integration), or
    • HealthShare
    Search for and select the preferred specialist. Their contact details will populate automatically.
  7. If there are relevant investigations to include (e.g., pathology results, imaging), you can attach these within the referral letter. They will be automatically included at the bottom of the letter.
  8. Once you are satisfied with each letter, click Save to BP. The letter will be saved to Correspondence Out in Best Practice.
  9. From Correspondence Out, you can:
    • Review the letter
    • Print and sign it
    • Email it directly to the specialist
    • Provide a copy to the patient
    • Edit it further as needed
Pro Tip: There is no need to copy and paste content between systems. Complete your referral letters entirely within Lyrebird Health and send them directly from Correspondence Out in Best Practice — saving two to three minutes per referral letter.

Key Benefits of This Workflow

  • Generate multiple referral letters in a single step — no repetition
  • Each letter is automatically tailored to the relevant specialty
  • No manual copy-pasting or recall required
  • Specialist contact details populated directly from Best Practice or HealthShare
  • Investigations can be attached and included automatically
  • All letters saved directly to Correspondence Out, ready to send

What Gets Automatically Included

  • Specialty-specific clinical information from the consultation transcript
  • Patient demographics and relevant medical context
  • Referral reason and clinical findings relevant to each recipient
  • Specialist contact details (via Best Practice or HealthShare)
  • Any investigations selected for inclusion
